# Datasheet: tuncall speech data

This datasheet follows the questions of *Datasheets for Datasets* (Gebru et al.) for every
source of tuncall's ASR training mix and both frozen test sets (P-10). The tables between
`<!-- … -->` markers are generated by `scripts/datasheet.py` from the text export of
`scripts/source_stats.py` (D-16b); edit the prose, never the tables.

```bash
uv run python scripts/datasheet.py
```

## Motivation

**Why does this data exist?** To train and evaluate an automatic speech recognition (ASR)
model for **Tunisian Arabic (Darija)**, the first stage of a call-analysis pipeline. No public
Tunisian call-center audio exists, so the project combines public Tunisian speech corpora
instead of recording its own (D-08v2, ADR 0006).

**Who assembled it?** The tuncall project. Nothing here is newly recorded: each source is a
public corpus read at a pinned Hub revision by an adapter in `src/tuncall/data/sources/`
(D-16). The two noisy-label sources (D-12d) will be the only data re-hosted, in private
derived repos (#39).

**Test sets.** test-general is LinTO's test split (D-14, D-14a) and test-domain is SLURP-TN's
test split (D-13a, D-14b). Both are frozen: their configs record file hashes and a
per-utterance digest, and evaluation refuses to run on any mismatch. Neither is used for
training or tuning. The validation splits of SLURP-TN and TEDxTN are not trained on either
(D-12c); which one Phase 3 tunes on is open (D-17).

**Overlap.** `docs/data/dedupe.md` has the full text-overlap matrix (D-16). Train clips whose
normalized text appears in any test or validation split are listed in
`configs/data/exclude.txt` and never trained on (D-16e). Sentences repeated inside a train
set are kept: the same sentence from different speakers is useful training data.

**Excluded source.** `Fares11/senior_tunisian_voice` was dropped: all 5,377 clips are copies of
LinTO's TunSwitchCS subset (D-12f).

## Collection process

None of the audio was collected by this project. As far as the upstream cards say:
LinTO gathers existing corpora and segmented public YouTube content; SLURP-TN speakers
recorded task-oriented requests; Omnilingual speakers answered open prompts; TEDxTN is public TEDx talks with professional transcripts. Speaker consent and
rights are as stated by each upstream dataset; this project adds no consent of its own.

## Preprocessing and labeling

- **Audio:** decoded, averaged to mono and resampled to 16 kHz with soxr (D-14a). LinTO
  recordings are cut into their timestamped segments, one clip per segment (D-16a).
- **Text fields:** LinTO `transcript`, SLURP-TN `tun_transcription`, Omnilingual
  `raw_text`, TEDxTN `sentence` (D-16a).
- **Omnilingual tags:** non-speech tags such as `<laugh>` and `<unintelligible>` are
  stripped; 51 clips left with no text are skipped (D-12g).
- **Script convention:** everything is written in Arabic script (D-10). Latin-script
  French/English words are transliterated with the owner-reviewed lexicon v1.1
  (`configs/data/lexicon.tsv`, D-10a, D-16d), applied identically to train and test.
- **Scoring:** references and hypotheses go through `normalize_arabic` before WER/CER (D-10).
- **Raw data** stays upstream at the pinned revisions in `configs/data/train-mix.yaml`.

## Known issues

- **No speaker ids** for LinTO, SLURP-TN and TEDxTN, so speaker overlap between their train
  and test splits can't be ruled out (D-13a). Scores may be optimistic for those speakers.
- **Noisy-label cleaning** (D-12h): Deep Confessions lost 52 of 37,156 clips (44 looping
  transcripts, 7 over 25 characters per second, 1 empty); its card in the derived repo has
  the counts. Its transcripts are machine-made and many carry diacritics (D-10c).
- **Clips over 30 s** (see the table) exceed Whisper's 30 s input window; Phase 3 must drop
  or split them.
- **Uneven sources:** LinTO is about two thirds of the training audio, and two YouTube
  channels alone (AbdelAzizErwi, HamzaBaloumiElMohakek) are about 36 h of it.
- **TEDxTN mirror provenance:** the upstream TEDxTN release ships annotations without
  audio; the mirror's audio source isn't documented.
- **Baseline contamination:** 3.5% of eligible test-general utterances appear verbatim in
  FarukSTT's training data, 0% of test-domain (D-12b, README).
- One TEDxTN train clip has empty text.

## Uses

- **Intended:** training and evaluating Tunisian Darija ASR within this project, and the
  with/without ablations of Phase 3.
- **Not intended:** speaker identification, or any use that conflicts with an upstream
  license. SLURP-TN and TEDxTN are non-commercial and no-derivatives.

## Distribution

The project doesn't redistribute any source: adapters read each one from its own Hub repo at
a pinned revision. Licenses were set aside while choosing sources for quality and are
reviewed before any model or dataset release (P-04a).

## Maintenance

Adding a source is one adapter and one line in `configs/data/train-mix.yaml` (D-16). After the
Colab export is re-run (`scripts/source_stats.py`), regenerate the dedupe report and
exclusions (`scripts/dedupe_report.py`), then this datasheet's tables
(`scripts/datasheet.py`). The test sets never change.
